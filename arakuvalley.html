About Araku Valley

Araku Valley is a beautiful hill destination in Andhra Pradesh, surrounded by lush green mountains, coffee plantations, waterfalls and forests. It is known for its peaceful atmosphere, scenic landscapes and rich tribal heritage.

Top Tourist Places

🌄 Araku Hills

Enjoy breathtaking views of the green Eastern Ghats and peaceful mountain landscapes.

💧 Katiki Waterfalls

A beautiful natural waterfall surrounded by greenery, located near the Borra Caves area.

🌳 Borra Caves

Explore fascinating limestone cave formations and spectacular natural surroundings.

🌿 Padmapuram Gardens

Relax among beautiful gardens and enjoy the greenery and peaceful environment.

☕ Araku Coffee

Experience the famous coffee-growing region and learn about the local coffee culture.

🪘 Tribal Culture

Discover the traditions, crafts, lifestyle and cultural heritage of the local tribal communities.
