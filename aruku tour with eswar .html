About Araku Valley

Araku Valley is a beautiful hill destination in Andhra Pradesh, India. It is famous for its green valleys, coffee plantations, waterfalls, forests and tribal culture.


The journey to Araku through the Eastern Ghats is one of the major attractions for visitors. Araku is an excellent destination for nature lovers, photographers and tourists.
